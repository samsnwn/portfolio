A real client site, running in production. Besides the design and build, most of the work went into making it robust:

- **Phased hardening plan.** Every change went through its own pull request that had to pass lint, typecheck, tests and build, then be checked on a preview deployment before going to production.
- **Abuse protection.** Rate limiting backed by Redis and bot protection on the contact flow.
- **Environment contract.** Environment variables are validated with Zod schemas, so a misconfigured deployment fails at startup instead of in front of a visitor.
- **End-to-end tests** with Playwright, including automated accessibility checks with axe.
