## The product

You create decks, or let AI generate cards from a topic, then study with a spaced-repetition scheduler that decides when each card comes back. The web and mobile apps keep working offline and sync when you reconnect.

## How it's built

A pnpm monorepo with `apps/` (API, web, mobile, landing page) and shared `packages/`: database schema, API client, an offline database, the study algorithm, i18n and shared schemas. The whole stack starts with one `docker compose up`, and database migrations run automatically.

## My part

I was the team's top committer and wrote the largest share of the web app, about twice as much as any teammate.

- **Web app.** The dashboard and learning statistics, deck and card management, the AI playground for generating cards, sign-in, sign-up and password reset with protected-route guards, onboarding, and the offline database and app store that keep everything working without a connection. Plus the component and route tests.
- **Design system.** Moved the web app onto the brand palette and design-system tokens.
- **Backend.** Authentication, including password-reset emails, the offline sync store, gamification, and the AI gateway (Groq as the provider with OpenRouter as a fallback, daily quotas per user, moderation re-checks).
- **Observability.** Prometheus metrics for AI requests, tokens and errors, with alerts on failing AI jobs.
- **Shared packages.** The offline database and the translations (i18n).
